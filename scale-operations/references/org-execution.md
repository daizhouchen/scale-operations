# Organization Execution

Use this file for team building, hiring, role clarity, leadership cadence, performance, and execution quality.

## Organization Diagnosis

Execution problems usually come from one of five gaps:

- Priority gap: teams do not know what matters most.
- Ownership gap: many people help but nobody owns the result.
- Capability gap: the team lacks skills, experience, or tools.
- Capacity gap: workload exceeds realistic throughput.
- Rhythm gap: goals, reviews, decisions, and feedback do not reinforce execution.

Name the gap before recommending hiring, process, or meetings.

## Role and Ownership Design

For each important operating outcome, define:

- Outcome owner.
- Decision rights.
- Required collaborators.
- Escalation threshold.
- Success metric.
- Review cadence.

Use responsibility models as a clarity tool, not as a spreadsheet ritual. If two people can veto a decision without a tie-breaker, the design is incomplete.

## Hiring and Team Building

Before hiring, specify:

- Which bottleneck the role removes.
- Which metric should improve.
- Which decisions the role will own.
- Which work will stop being done by founders or managers.
- What good looks like at 30, 60, and 90 days.

Use hiring scorecards to compare candidates against outcomes, not personality impressions.

## Leadership Cadence

Managers need a small set of loops:

- 1:1s for context, coaching, obstacles, and feedback.
- Weekly team review for priorities, execution risks, and metrics.
- Monthly performance and capacity review.
- Quarterly org design and capability review.

Do not turn every issue into a meeting. If the issue is recurring, fix the system that keeps producing it.

## Performance and Accountability

Good accountability has:

- Clear expectation.
- Observable behavior or output.
- Timely feedback.
- Support or resources.
- Consequence or decision if performance does not change.

Separate willingness, capability, and clarity. Each needs a different response.

## Metrics

- Goal completion quality.
- Decision latency.
- Manager span and escalation load.
- Time to productivity for new hires.
- Role vacancy impact.
- Rework caused by unclear ownership.
- Employee load or burnout indicators.
- Performance distribution and calibration outcomes.

## Failure Signals

- Founder or COO remains the default decision owner.
- People are busy but top priorities do not move.
- Hiring increases coordination cost faster than output.
- Performance issues are discussed but not addressed directly.
- Teams confuse collaboration with consensus.
- The same cross-functional conflict repeats every week.
