## Test 1: Cost pressure while revenue is growing

Prompt: "Our revenue is still growing, but costs are rising faster and runway is shrinking. Help me reduce costs without hurting growth."

Expected behavior:
- Diagnose whether the issue is margin, cash timing, productivity, growth quality, or complexity.
- Protect growth-critical spend before proposing reductions.
- Segment costs and customers instead of recommending across-the-board cuts.
- Include owners, weekly cash/budget cadence, metrics, and countermetrics.

Result: Pass. The skill points to `economic-control.md`, which prioritizes economic diagnosis, protected spend, cost-control ladder, budget rhythm, and countermetrics such as churn, quality, employee load, and sales velocity.

## Test 2: Churn increase

Prompt: "Customer churn went up in the last two months. What should our ops team do?"

Expected behavior:
- Segment customers before proposing campaigns.
- Identify lifecycle point and promise/experience gap.
- Convert customer data into recovery actions and feedback loops.
- Track leading behavior before lagging retention.

Result: Pass. The skill points to `customer-intelligence.md`, which requires segmentation, lifecycle diagnosis, root-cause hypotheses, targeted experiments, and operating loops.

## Test 3: Team expansion slowed execution

Prompt: "We doubled the team, but execution became slower and every decision comes back to the founder."

Expected behavior:
- Treat the issue as an ownership/rhythm problem before hiring or adding tools.
- Clarify outcome owners, decision rights, escalation thresholds, and review cadence.
- Include decision latency and founder escalation load as metrics.

Result: Pass. The skill points to `org-execution.md` and `operating-system.md`, which emphasize ownership gaps, decision rights, cadence, and decision latency.
